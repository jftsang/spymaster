Privacy policy

Spymaster is a small, non-commercial hobby project. This policy explains what information the web app handles and why.

What we collect

  • A username. The name you type to play. It is shown to other players and does not have to be your real name.
  • A session cookie. When you connect, the server sets a single session cookie containing a random identifier. It keeps you logged in across reloads and reconnects. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax and is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Game state. Your moves, scores and current game are held in the server's memory while you play.

What we do not collect

  • No passwords, email addresses or accounts.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Third parties

The page loads the Bootstrap stylesheet from the jsDelivr CDN. Your browser requests that file directly from jsDelivr, which may receive your IP address as part of serving it.

Retention

Usernames and game state live in the server's memory and are discarded when the server restarts or the session ends. The session cookie stays in your browser until you clear it.

Your choices

You can play under any name you like, clear the session cookie at any time, or simply stop using the site.
